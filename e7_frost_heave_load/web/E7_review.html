▲ Decision needed before buying

What should E7 measure?

  • Frost heave: raise saturation to Sr ≥ 0.85, or supply water from below (open system). Then heave of 0.1–1 mm is realistic.
  • Thaw settlement under load at Sr 0.3–0.5: keep the sample; expect tens of µm and rely on the blank-run correction.

Before the first real cycle

  1. Blank run: Al dummy instead of sand, one full cycle; fit drift against cell temperature.
  2. Calibrate the camera with shims 0.1 / 0.5 / 1.0 mm under a flat plate.
  3. Measure the freezer: rig 598 mm vs ≈ 650 mm inside.
  4. Confirm LVDT operation at −10 °C with RDP / Elkome.
  5. Program: 24 h at −10 °C, 24 h at +10 °C (thaw takes ≈ 13 h).
